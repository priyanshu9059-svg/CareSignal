# SIH demonstration

Start both servers using README.md. Open http://127.0.0.1:3000. Use a fresh database for first-login consent, or reopen Consent & privacy to demonstrate it. Every page is marked Prototype / Synthetic Data.

1. Choose the victim demo account and sign in. Select Hindi or Hinglish using the language menu.
2. Accept wellbeing consent, and optionally voice processing. Save preferences.
3. Start check-in. Select difficulty levels for mood, fear, sleep, daily tasks, avoidance and legal stress. Report threat=yes and safe=no for the escalation scenario.
4. Enter: “Mujhe bahut darr lag raha hai. Unhone mere bhai ko phir se dhamki di hai aur mujhe court jaane se dar lag raha hai. Neend nahi aati.”
5. Optionally record audio. Permit the microphone. Stop recording and review/type the transcript. Where browser ASR is absent or fails, text still works and audio measurements can still be attached. The server does not pretend to transcribe unavailable speech.
6. Submit. A historical assessment and staff alert are stored. The participant sees a supportive receipt, not a frightening numerical priority.
7. Sign out; sign in as officer. Open Alerts & review, acknowledge the new alert, then open AT-20481. Inspect What changed, three risk dimensions, explanation contributions, history, case events and voice baseline detail.
8. Choose a same-district counsellor and explicitly confirm Counsellor follow-up, Safety/protection review and Legal support review. Internal notes remain staff-only. Schedule a future follow-up date/time.
9. Mark contact and intervention progress, including an observed outcome note where appropriate.
10. Return as participant. The support journey shows assignments and follow-up. Submit a new check-in with lower difficulty and improved text, or enable Demo mode and select improvement. It is recalculated, not hardcoded to 49.
11. Return as officer/counsellor. The updated trajectory and intervention outcome panel show observed changes. Historical seed AT-20481 includes 32→39→47→58→74→78→63→49, with an intervention before improvement.
12. Use state and national accounts for aggregate oversight. Use admin for measured model metrics and audit logs.

Demo scenarios append fresh input-derived assessments to the participant's own case. They do not rewrite history or silently resolve threats in case records. Exact illustrative scores from the brief are not forced. Strong safety signals can maintain high priority even after distress improves; this is why the three dimensions are separate.

Data that pre-exists in the seed is clearly synthetic. Real-world benefit, intervention causality and clinical accuracy are not claimed. External notifications and service adapters are simulated only.
